Good questions. Clear answers.

Know what to expect before you decide to improve your website or grow your online presence.

Can you improve the website I already have?+

Yes. We can review your current site and recommend practical improvements to its design, mobile experience, speed, search setup, and enquiry or booking flow. If a rebuild makes more sense, we’ll explain why before you decide.

How can you help more customers find my business?+

We can improve your site’s structure and content for search, then use Google or Meta ads to reach relevant audiences. SEO typically takes time, and ad results depend on your offer, audience, budget, and competition.

Can you guarantee more traffic, enquiries, or sales?+

No one can honestly guarantee a specific result. We improve the parts we can control—visibility setup, message clarity, mobile usability, and the path to contact or buy—and can use analytics to see what to improve next.

What is included in the $49.99 starter website?+

It starts with a one-page, mobile-friendly site for a simple business introduction, contact links, basic on-page SEO setup, and one revision. Extra pages, custom features, content creation, hosting, domain, and third-party tools may cost extra; we’ll agree on any additional costs before work begins.

Will I know the full price before you start?+

Yes. We’ll confirm the project scope and price first. If you request work beyond the agreed scope, we’ll explain the cost and get your approval before proceeding. Domain, hosting, advertising, and third-party services may have separate provider fees.

Can you build AI tools or automate a business task?+

Yes. We can explore AI assistants, workflow automation, integrations, and custom web apps for tasks such as answering common questions, qualifying enquiries, or moving information between tools. We’ll first check that the proposed solution fits the problem.

Do you work with businesses outside the Netherlands?+

Yes. LexDev is based in the Netherlands and works with clients worldwide. We can discuss your goals remotely and agree on the best way to communicate throughout the project.
