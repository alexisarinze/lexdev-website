Questions before you get started.

A few things business owners usually want to know before starting a digital project.

How long does a website take? +

It depends on the size and functionality. A focused landing page can be completed much faster than a multi-page website, online store or custom application. We'll agree on the scope and timeline before work begins.

I already have a website. Can you improve it? +

Absolutely. We can review what's working, identify what may be costing you customers, improve the design and usability, speed up the experience and rebuild areas that need a stronger foundation.

Will my website work on phones? +

Yes. Your customers use different devices, so responsive behavior across phones, tablets and desktops is part of the development process.

Can you connect WhatsApp, payments, bookings and other tools? +

Yes. We can integrate the tools your business already relies on, depending on their technical requirements. Tell us what you use and what you want the website to do.

Can you build an AI agent for my business? +

Yes. We can explore AI assistants for customer support, lead handling, internal knowledge, repetitive tasks and other workflows where automation could make sense.

What happens after my website goes live? +

Launch isn't necessarily the finish line. We can continue with updates, improvements, SEO, performance optimization and new features as your business needs change.
